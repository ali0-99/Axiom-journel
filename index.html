How the Payment Gateway Is Used

Axiom Trading Journal offers a free tier and an optional paid tier with additional features. Here is exactly how a customer pays and how the payment gateway is used during checkout.
1

Customer creates a free account using email/password or Google Sign-In, and starts using the core trade-journaling features at no cost.

2

Customer clicks "Upgrade" inside their account dashboard to view paid-plan features and pricing.

3

Customer is directed to a secure checkout powered by our payment gateway partner, where they can pay by debit/credit card, JazzCash, easypaisa, or bank transfer.

4

Payment gateway confirms the transaction and notifies our backend server of a successful payment.

5

Customer's account is automatically upgraded and premium features are unlocked instantly — no manual steps, no physical delivery, no waiting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the trading journal.

Is Axiom Trading Journal free to use?
Yes. You can create a free account and use the core trade-logging, pip/RR/P&L calculations, and equity curve features at no cost. A paid plan with additional features is optional.

Can Forex traders in Pakistan use this journal?
Yes. Axiom Trading Journal is built and operated in Pakistan and works for any Forex or financial trader worldwide, including those using local payment methods like JazzCash and easypaisa for paid upgrades.

What does the trading journal calculate automatically?
Pip movement, risk-to-reward ratio, profit and loss, and hold time are calculated automatically from the entry/exit prices and position size you enter for each trade.

Can I track multiple trading accounts?
Yes. You can track demo, live, and prop-firm accounts separately, all inside a single login, and export your data to Excel/CSV or JSON at any time.
